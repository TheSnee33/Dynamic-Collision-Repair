OUR STORY

We Are Driven By Values

In 1979, Robin Roberts opened the doors to Dynamic Collision Repair on State Street in Provo with a simple belief: treat every customer like a neighbor, and do every repair as if it were your own car. That promise has never changed. What started as a small family shop has grown into one of Utah County's most trusted names in collision repair — all while staying true to the values Robin built it on.

Today, the Roberts family continues to run Dynamic Collision with the same hands-on commitment. We don't cut corners, we don't upsell, and we stand behind every job with a lifetime warranty. When your car needs repair, you deserve a shop that cares as much about getting it right as you do.
