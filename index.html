Our Story

A Family Legacy Since 1979

In 1979, a 21-year-old Robin Roberts opened a small auto body shop on State Street in Provo with nothing but a set of tools, a work ethic forged in Utah's mountain air, and a simple promise: treat every car like it was your own.

Over four decades later, that promise hasn't changed. Now with Brandon Roberts leading operations and insurance relations, Dynamic Collision Repair continues to be the shop Utah County families trust most. We've repaired thousands of vehicles — from fender benders to total rebuilds — and every single one leaves with a lifetime warranty.
