Our Story

Over 46 years of family-owned quality in the heart of Provo

OUR HISTORY

Built on Family Values Since 1979

In 1979, a 21-year-old Robin Roberts opened the doors of Dynamic Collision Repair on South State Street in Provo, Utah. With nothing but determination, a set of tools, and a commitment to honest work, he set out to build something that would last.

Four decades and thousands of satisfied customers later, that small shop has become one of Utah County's most trusted names in auto body repair. Still family-owned, still on State Street, still delivering the same quality and integrity that Robin built the business on from day one.

Today, the next generation of the Roberts family, including Brandon Roberts, continues the tradition, bringing modern collision repair techniques together with old-school craftsmanship. When you bring your car to Dynamic Collision, you're not just another ticket number — you're part of the family.
