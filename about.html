Our Story

How It Started

A Dream Built on Hard Work

In 1979, a 21-year-old Robin Roberts opened a small auto body shop on South State Street in Provo, Utah. With nothing but a set of tools, an unshakeable work ethic, and a handshake promise to treat every vehicle like his own, Robin set out to build something that would last.

What started as a one-man operation quickly grew into Provo's most trusted name in collision repair. Word spread through Utah County — Robin's work was honest, his prices were fair, and his repairs were built to last. Over four decades later, that reputation hasn't just survived — it's stronger than ever.
