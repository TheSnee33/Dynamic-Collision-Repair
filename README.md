## About

Dynamic Collision Repair is a family-owned auto body and collision repair shop located at **1671 South State Street, Provo, UT 84606**. Founded by Robin Roberts in 1979, the shop has served Utah County for over 46 years with honest pricing, quality craftsmanship, and a lifetime warranty on every repair.

## Services

- 🔧 **Collision Repair** — Frame-to-finish restoration
- 🚗 **Auto Body Repair** — Dent removal, panel replacement, hail damage
- 🎨 **Paint & Refinishing** — Computerized color matching, full/partial paint
- 📐 **Frame Alignment** — Computerized measuring and precision straightening
- 🔩 **Rim Repair** — Curb rash, bent rims, alloy and steel
- ✅ **Free Estimates** — Walk-ins welcome, no obligation
