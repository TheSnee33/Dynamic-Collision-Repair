Auto Paint & Refinishing Services

A seamless paint finish is essential after collision or exterior body repairs. At Dynamic Collision Repair, we use advanced computerized color-matching technology to precisely duplicate and match your vehicle's factory color when repainting new replacement parts and repaired body panels.

Our downdraft spray booth ensures a clean, dust-free environment so replacement bumpers, fenders, hoods, and doors blend imperceptibly with your vehicle's existing original finish. We use premium automotive basecoats and high-durability clear coats designed to provide long-lasting UV resistance and weather protection.

  • Computerized factory color matching
  • Replacement panel paint & blending
  • Clear coat restoration
  • Scratch, chip & scuff repair
