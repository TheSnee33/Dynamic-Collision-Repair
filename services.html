Collision & Auto Body Repair — Provo, Utah

Whether you've been in a minor fender bender or experienced major collision damage, the team at Dynamic Collision Repair has the expertise to restore your vehicle to its pre-accident condition. Since 1979, we've helped thousands of drivers across Provo and Utah County get back on the road safely with meticulous collision and auto body craftsmanship that meets or exceeds manufacturer specifications.

From structural frame straightening, fender and bumper replacement, to paintless dent repair and hail damage restoration, our skilled technicians handle complete vehicle body restoration under one roof. We work directly with all major insurance companies to streamline your claims process and back all our bodywork with a lifetime warranty for as long as you own the vehicle.

  • Complete collision restoration & structural repair
  • Dent removal, panel replacement & hail repair
  • Direct insurance claims handling
  • Lifetime Warranty on all body & collision craftsmanship

Auto Paint & Refinishing — 7-Year Warranty

A seamless paint finish is essential after collision or exterior body repairs. At Dynamic Collision Repair, we use computerized color-matching technology to precisely duplicate and match your vehicle's factory color when repainting new replacement parts and blending exterior panels.

Our climate-controlled downdraft spray booth ensures a spotless, factory-grade finish free of dust and contaminants. To protect your investment and withstand Utah's extreme sun and seasonal road salt, our paint refinishing is backed by a comprehensive Seven-Year Paint Warranty — matching new-vehicle factory standards for lasting durability and peace of mind.

  • Computerized factory color matching & panel blending
  • Down-draft spray booth for flawless application
  • Premium UV-resistant basecoat & clear coat
  • 7-Year Warranty on all paint & refinishing work

Mechanical Work — Brakes, Rotors & Suspension

Collision impact often damages steering and suspension components, while regular driving requires trustworthy brake maintenance. At Dynamic Collision Repair, our mechanical specialists provide precision service focused strictly on essential vehicle handling and stopping power: brakes, rotors, and suspension systems.

Whether your vehicle needs new ceramic brake pads, rotor replacement or resurfacing, struts, shocks, or control arm adjustments after an accident, we provide upfront quotes and dependable repairs. (Please note: Our mechanical division specializes strictly in brakes, rotors, and suspension components — we do not offer oil changes).

  • Brake pad replacement & rotor resurfacing / replacement
  • Struts, shocks, control arms & steering suspension repair
  • Post-collision mechanical safety & alignment inspections
  • Transparent, honest estimates with no unnecessary upsells
