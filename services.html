Frame Alignment & Structural Repair in Utah County

Proper frame alignment is critical for your vehicle's safety and handling. Even a minor collision can shift the frame or unibody structure, leading to uneven tire wear, poor handling, and compromised crash protection. Dynamic Collision Repair uses computerized frame-measuring systems to detect even the slightest misalignment and restore your vehicle's structural geometry to exact factory specifications.

Our certified technicians perform precision frame straightening, unibody repairs, and structural welding using manufacturer-approved methods. Every frame repair undergoes rigorous verification to ensure it meets safety compliance standards — because your family's safety is never something we compromise on.

  • Computerized frame measuring
  • Unibody & full-frame repair
  • Structural welding
  • Safety compliance verification
