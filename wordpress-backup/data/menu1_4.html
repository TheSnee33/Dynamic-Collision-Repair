FAQ

Q: My insurance company tells me I need to take my car to one of their “Preferred Shops” is this true?

A:Absolutely not! Insurance companies tell you this because their “Preferred Shops” give them discounts on parts, labor, etc. so that the repairs cost the insurance company less money. Which makes sense, but is the insurance company looking out for their best interest or yours? I think you can figure that out. Taking your car to a shop that doesn't work for your insurance company can actually be more beneficial because the discounts that would have gone to them can go to you instead.

 

Q: But they say the work won't be guaranteed unless I take it where they tell me.. what about that?

 A: This is just another ploy to get you to take it where they want. The shop guarantees the work not the insurance company, so as long as the shop you take it to offers a lifetime guarantee, it really doesn't matter which shop you choose.

 

Q: My car isn't driveable or I don't feel safe driving it, what should I do?

 A: We have a towing service available for customers so you have nothing to worry about. Just let us know where the car is at and when we can pick it up and we'll take care of it from there. Most insurance policies have tow coverage as well, so if you have that coverage, it won't even cost you a dime.
